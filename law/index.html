Before you start

Common questions

We already use an answering service. Is this useful?

Possibly. Show us one handoff from your service to intake. If your tools already assign an owner, flag exceptions and record the return call, we’ll say so. A small configuration change may be all you need.

What does the caller hear?

Your firm approves the exact opening, the AI disclosure and the way a caller can reach a person. The pilot collects only the details needed for a return call. Your legal team handles advice, case evaluation and decisions about representation.

What if a request fails to reach the team?

We agree on a backup route and test a deliberate delivery failure before launch. An unresolved request stays visible. Staff can switch forwarding back to the original route.

What do we need for the first conversation?

Your intake lead and a description of one recent handoff are enough. Bring the roles and steps. Leave caller names, medical details and case information out.
