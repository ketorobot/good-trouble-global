Good to know

Before you start.

Already use an answering service?

If it already gives each request an owner, flags delivery failures and shows who tried to call back, you may not need this.

What does the caller hear?

Your firm approves the script, including the upfront AI disclosure and how to reach a person. We only take callback details. Your firm handles advice and case decisions.

What if a request doesn’t reach intake?

We test a backup route before launch and agree on who handles failed deliveries. Your staff can switch back to your current routing.
