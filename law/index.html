Straight Answers

Is this replacing my intake staff?

No. It makes them superhuman. Your team keeps every conversation that needs judgment and empathy — the platform handles the 2am calls, the fifth follow-up text, and the hundred small touches nobody has time for. Staff spend their day on the leads most likely to sign, not on chasing voicemail.

Does this respect bar advertising and communication rules?

The platform is built with law-firm communication rules in mind — consent-based messaging, opt-outs, and review requests structured around how bar advertising rules treat testimonials. Your firm retains final say on all outbound language, and we configure every module to your jurisdiction's requirements with you.

How long does setup take?

Days, not months. Core modules — intake, follow-up, and reviews — go live first so you see results immediately, and the rest of the system builds out from there. We handle the technical work end to end; your team's involvement is telling us how your firm runs, not learning new software.

What makes this different from tools we've already tried?

Tools are fixed: you configure them once and they do the same thing forever, whether it's working or not. This is a different category — an intelligent system that keeps learning what produces signed cases for your firm and realigns around it. And it keeps growing: new modules ship continuously to every subscriber.

Do we need technical staff to run it?

No. We build it, run it, and keep improving it — that's what the subscription is. There's nothing to install, no software for your paralegals to master, and no IT hire required. If your team can read a dashboard and answer a phone, they can use everything the platform does.
