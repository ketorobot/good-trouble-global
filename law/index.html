Will we have to change our software?＋

We start with what you already use. We’ll check what can connect to your phone system, calendar, and case management software, then walk you through the options.

What if we already have an intake team?＋

We build around them. That might mean helping with after-hours inquiries, preparing notes before a call, or keeping track of follow-ups. We’ll work out where the help is actually useful.

Who decides what the AI says?＋

Your firm does. We work with you on the questions it asks, the answers it can give, and when it should hand the conversation to a person. Legal advice and decisions about taking a case stay with your team.

Can we start with the thing we need most?＋

Yes. If follow-up is the problem, we can start there. If your team needs help with marketing or daily admin, we can focus on that. We’ll build around what would make a difference for you.
