About Good Trouble

The software should adapt to you.
Not the other way around.

Most business software asks you to change how you operate. Learn its workflows. Work around its limitations. Add another dashboard. Add another login. Add another piece of software your team eventually stops using.

We think that model is backwards.

Good Trouble builds intelligent systems that learn how your practice works, understand what matters to you, and reshape themselves around your priorities.

Not another tool to manage.

A system that helps manage the business.

We started with a simple question.

What if software had judgment?

Not just automation. Not “if this happens, do that.”

A system that understands the difference between a new lead and a longtime client. That knows which clients are worth reactivating today. That understands how your practice communicates, what your staff handles well, where things routinely fall through the cracks, and what the owner actually cares about.

That was the idea behind Good Trouble.

After a decade working around med spa growth, marketing, client acquisition, retention, and operations, one thing became obvious:

Med spas weren’t suffering from a lack of software. They had plenty of it.

Booking software. CRMs. Email platforms. Texting tools. Review platforms. Marketing systems. Automations.

The problem was that none of it was thinking together.

So we started building something different.

Intelligence + adaptability.

That combination is the entire point.

A traditional system does what it was programmed to do. A Good Trouble system is built around your practice and continues changing with it.

  • If filling cancellations becomes the priority, the system can focus there.
  • If your biggest opportunity becomes dormant clients, it can shift toward reactivation.
  • If you’re launching memberships, opening another location, changing providers, promoting a new treatment, or simply deciding that something should work differently — the system changes with you.

Your business isn’t static.
Your software shouldn’t be either.

One practice. One connected intelligence.

Good Trouble can sit across the parts of the business that normally operate separately:

Front Desk

Answer questions, capture inquiries, handle routine conversations, and know when a human should take over.

Client Reactivation

Find opportunities already sitting inside your database and intelligently bring the right clients back.

Campaigns

Build and execute outreach around your actual clients, treatments, timing, and goals.

Reviews

Understand client sentiment before deciding what happens next.

Memberships

Identify opportunities, support enrollment, and help keep members engaged.

Follow-Up

Make sure the conversations and actions that should happen actually happen.

Analytics

Turn everything happening across the practice into information you can actually use.

They can work independently. The bigger idea is what happens when they work together.

We aren’t trying to build another SaaS company.

Software companies usually build one product and sell the same thing thousands of times. Agencies usually sell human labor.

Good Trouble sits somewhere new.

We build the underlying technology once, then shape its intelligence around each business. That means two med spas using Good Trouble don’t necessarily get the same system. They shouldn’t.

Different owners have different priorities.

Different teams have different strengths.

Different locations attract different clients.

Different businesses have different problems worth solving.

The technology should understand that.

Built with you, not handed to you.

There isn’t a giant implementation manual.

  • We learn how the practice works.
  • We determine where intelligence and automation can actually make a difference.
  • We connect what should be connected.
  • Then we keep improving it.

You don’t need to become an AI expert. You shouldn’t have to become a software expert either.

That’s our job.
