Before we start

A few good questions.

Do I need to buy everything?+

No. We can start with one part of the business or a wider setup. Your quote lists exactly what is included.

Do I need new booking software?+

We review the tools you already use and how to connect them. Any new tools, access requirements, or manual steps are covered in the plan.

What about software and message costs?+

Your proposal separates our work from any software, message usage, or other third-party costs. Ad spend is identified separately when a campaign needs it.

How long does setup take?+

The launch plan depends on the work and the tools involved. You get a timeline in your proposal before work starts.

What happens after launch?+

We agree on support, improvements, and review of results. Ongoing fees, the initial term, cancellation, and data handoff are set out in writing.
